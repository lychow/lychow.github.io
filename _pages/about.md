I am a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Associate Professor
Ming-Chang Yang. My research focuses on using FPGA-based platforms, specifically OpenSSD, to prototype and validate emerging SSD architectures, such as Zoned Namespace (ZNS) and Dual-Interface SSDs.
